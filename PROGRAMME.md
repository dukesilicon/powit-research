## Witness Event

Select a safe, bounded physical occurrence with an unambiguous observation question and adequate separately recorded reference evidence. Avoid elections, allegations about identifiable people, or other high-stakes claims for this first funded operational study. Select exact participant count, locality, compensation and windows before recruitment. Participation must be voluntary, with a practical withdrawal and data-handling explanation that distinguishes pre-collection withdrawal from immutable-record retention decisions.

Geolocation alone is insufficient; livestream viewing is not qualifying physical witnessing. Such a review concerns provenance/authenticity criteria, not whether every associated event claim is true. ## Operational and adversarial examination

Record denominators for enrollments, accepted commitments, attempted and matching reveals, admitted Observations, non-reveals, rejected actions, completed Records and successful external replays. Record task completion time, comprehension difficulties and operator interventions. Distinguish a participant's changed belief from an invalid attempt to overwrite a committed report.

Investigate repeated participation tokens, separately created accounts for one person, coordinated reports from distinct people, late intake, wrong openings, manipulated stored inputs, missing replay material, misleading location metadata, relayed footage and reused or altered evidence. Existing regression tests already exercise some computational cases; field tests must not be counted as already performed. Experiments involving deception require an agreed consent/debrief plan and must remain within the safe event.

Publish failures and the response to them. Operational adjustments can improve instructions, scheduling or inspection without changing the frozen kernel. Any required semantic correction is a separate decision; preserve old inputs and outputs. If the operational gate fails, use the final month to characterize and report the failure, and defer comparative claims rather than manufacture a successful trial.

## Comparative epistemic evaluation

The question is whether access to the Record changes judgments in a beneficial way after the operational process works. Predefine two presentations of matched underlying evidence: ordinary reports/material and the same evidence organized with PoWit provenance, admission and disagreement information. Randomize assessors or counterbalance disjoint cases so a reader does not see the same case twice. Match question wording, evidence content and time budget. If more evidence is supplied to one arm, label that comparison separately as a combined intervention.

Use reference facts established independently of witness consensus, preferably from a controlled occurrence and separately reviewed reference material. Keep reference adjudication separate from condition scoring. Mark genuinely unresolved claims as unresolved; exclude them from binary-accuracy scoring and examine appropriate uncertainty instead. Lock question set, adjudication, exclusion criteria and analysis before evaluation responses.

| Outcome | Proposed measurement | Interpretation constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Factual accuracy | Correct/incorrect judgments on reference-resolved propositions; report counts and abstentions separately | Reference quality is itself a limitation; no majority-derived answer key |
| Confidence calibration | Elicit probabilities for binary claims; Brier score = mean of (probability − reference outcome)²; show confidence versus observed correctness | Small samples cannot support fine calibration bins or broad population claims |
| Contradiction recognition | Detection of pre-identified incompatible accounts, with false-positive rate on compatible accounts | Detecting disagreement is different from resolving which witness is correct |
| Unwarranted certainty | Fraction of high-confidence errors using a threshold fixed before responses; confidence on deliberately underdetermined questions | Do not infer low error from mere abstention; report coverage and accuracy together |

Set feasible assessor and case counts after the operational gate within the selected recipient-evaluation envelope ($2,500 at full funding; $960 at reduced funding). The application promises a bounded pilot, not a powered definitive trial. Report effect estimates, uncertainty and all exclusions; avoid treating repeated judgments from one reader or one event as independent participants. Record readability, completion time and comprehension as secondary outcomes. The study may find no benefit or increased overconfidence; either result informs the central objective.
